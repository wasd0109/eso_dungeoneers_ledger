[b][size=5]Overview[/size][/b]
Dungeoneer's Ledger adds a progress ledger to the Dungeon Finder (Group > Dungeon Finder > Specific Dungeons). See at a glance which dungeons you have completed, which extra veteran achievements you have earned and which dungeons you have a quest for, then group, sort, search and select them in one click.

[b][size=5]Features[/size][/b]
[list]
[*]Completion icon next to every dungeon, from the dungeon's own achievement.
[*]Extra veteran markers: hard mode, speed run, no death, trifecta - lit once earned.
[*]Quest marker for dungeons you have a quest for (Undaunted pledges and other dungeon quests).
[*]Tooltip lists your other characters with a quest for the same dungeon.
[*]Group by completion or DLC / chapter; pin quest dungeons to the top.
[*]Sort, search by name, hide completed dungeons.
[*]Progress line, e.g. "Completed: Normal 27/41 - Veteran 18/41".
[*]One-click "Select quest dungeons" and "Select and queue" buttons.
[/list]

[b][size=5]Settings[/size][/b]
Settings > Add-Ons > Dungeoneer's Ledger, or [b]/dledger settings[/b]. Defaults in brackets.
[list]
[*][b]Show completion icon[/b] (on): the check box next to each dungeon; its tooltip names the achievement.
[*][b]Show completion dates in tooltips[/b] (on): adds the date an achievement was earned to the completion icon and marker tooltips, formatted by the game for your language.
[*][b]Show set collection progress in tooltips[/b] (on): adds "Set pieces collected: X / Y" to the completion icon tooltip, from the game's item set collections (through LibSets).
[*][b]Show extra achievement markers[/b] (on): markers for a veteran dungeon's extra achievements, grey until earned; the tooltip lists each tier.
[*][b]Hard mode marker[/b], [b]Speed run marker[/b], [b]No death marker[/b], [b]Trifecta marker[/b] (all on): show or hide each tier. Needs the main markers toggle.
[*][b]Show dungeons I have a quest for[/b] (on): marks dungeons matching an active quest of this character.
[*][b]Show which characters have quests for a dungeon[/b] (on): adds "Also active on: ..." to that tooltip.
[*][b]Remember completed pledges (done today)[/b] (on): records the Undaunted pledges your characters turn in and shows "Pledge completed today (character)" on the quest marker until the next daily reset (19:00 JST / 10:00 UTC).
[*][b]Pin dungeons with a quest to the top[/b] (off): own group "Dungeons with a quest" at the top of the NORMAL and VETERAN lists; works together with grouping.
[*][b]Select and queue veteran dungeons instead of normal[/b] (off): the buttons pick the veteran version of each dungeon whichever difficulty the finder shows. Off: they follow your current dungeon difficulty.
[*][b]Skip completed when selecting quest dungeons[/b] (off): the buttons leave out completed dungeons.
[*][b]Icon size[/b] (18, 12-32): size of all icons.
[*][b]Hide completed dungeons[/b] (off): removes completed dungeons from the list.
[*][b]Group dungeons by[/b] (None): None = game order; Completion = "Not completed" / "Completed"; DLC / chapter. Group headers show counts.
[*][b]Sort dungeons by[/b] (Game order): Game order, Name (A-Z), DLC, Completion, Completion date (newest first) or (oldest first), inside each list or group. Undated dungeons sort last.
[/list]

[b][size=5]Slash commands[/size][/b]
[list]
[*][b]/dledger[/b] or [b]/dungeoneersledger[/b]: usage
[*][b]/dledger settings[/b]: open the settings panel
[*][b]/dledger debug[/b]: print how each dungeon was matched and which quests were found
[*][b]/dledger debug on[/b] / [b]off[/b]: print the first error of each kind once
[/list]

[b][size=5]Buttons[/size][/b]
Two buttons sit beside the game's Join Queue button.
[list]
[*][b]Select quest dungeons[/b]: ticks the dungeons you have a quest for, for one difficulty.
[*][b]Select and queue[/b]: the same, then presses the game's own Join Queue.
[/list]
Safeguards: one click is one action; nothing is ever selected or queued automatically; the game's own checks apply (locked dungeons are skipped and listed in chat); nothing happens while you are queued, when the Group Finder is in use, or when you are grouped but not the leader; normal and veteran are never mixed; dungeons hidden by the search or hide-completed filter are never queued.

[b][size=5]Notes and limitations[/size][/b]
[list]
[*]Achievements are account-wide.
[*]"Also active on" only knows characters you have logged into with the addon.
[*]Today's Undaunted pledges are not visible to addons before you accept them; the game does not expose them. Only completed pledges can be remembered.
[*]Extra markers and the exact dungeon mapping depend on LibSets data; newer dungeons may fall back to name matching.
[*]Keyboard and mouse UI only. Remove the old DungeonCompletion addon if you had it.
[/list]
